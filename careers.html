Recruitment FAQs

Questions, answered.

How do I apply for a vacancy?

Open a vacancy to review its full description and deadline, then use Apply Now. While online submission is being configured, send your application documents to athinet72@gmail.com.

What file types can I submit?

The online application accepts PDF and DOCX documents, with a 5 MB limit per file and a 10 MB total limit.

Can I apply after the deadline?

No. Applications are not accepted for a vacancy after its deadline or after it is marked closed.

Will every applicant be contacted?

The recruitment team will review applications and contact shortlisted candidates.
